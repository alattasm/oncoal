# وصف الصور: يوسف والعملاق

**طريقة الاستخدام:**
- أرفق صورة يوسف (`images/yusuf-reference.png`) مع **كل** طلب.
- انسخ «الأسلوب الثابت» و«وصف الشخصيات» في كل مرة، وبعدها وصف الصفحة.
- بعد أول صورة تعجبك، أرفقها هي بعد مع كل طلب، عشان يبقى يوسف والعملاق بنفس الشكل في كل الصفحات.

**المقاسات:** صور الصفحات بالعرض (3:2 landscape)، والغلاف مربع (1:1).
**أسماء الملفات:** احفظ الصور في `images/` بأسماء `scene-01.png` … `scene-20.png`، والغلاف `cover.png`، وبعدها شغّل `node build.js`.

---

## الأسلوب الثابت (Style), انسخه كل مرة
```
Children's picture book illustration, warm soft digital painting with gentle textures, rich warm desert palette (sand, terracotta, palm green, sky blue), soft lighting, rounded friendly shapes, expressive faces, cozy and joyful mood, high detail but uncluttered, Saudi / Arabian Peninsula setting (mud-brick Najdi houses, date palms, rocky mountains). Keep main characters in the center of the frame, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```

## وصف الشخصيات, انسخه كل مرة
```
YUSUF (main character, use the attached reference image for his face and hair): a cheerful toddler boy about 3 years old, big round brown eyes, rosy cheeks, fluffy wavy messy brown hair, light warm skin. He always wears the same outfit: a royal-blue sweatshirt with a small bluebird sitting on a leafy branch printed on the chest, matching royal-blue jogger pants, small white sneakers. No brand logos on his clothes.
THE GIANT: a huge but kind-looking man, as tall as a palm tree, with a big bushy dark-brown beard, thick eyebrows, large warm eyes, tan skin, wearing a simple teal tunic with a dark belt and a gold buckle, barefoot, sandals optional. He looks scary only when frowning; otherwise gentle.
```

Book cover illustration, square. Yusuf stands in front, smiling and waving up at the friendly giant, who smiles and waves back.
